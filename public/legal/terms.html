Campfire — Internal Use Terms

Effective September 24, 2026.

Purpose and authorized use

Campfire is an internal business application operated by Howl Campfires, LLC (HOWL). HOWL permits authorized personnel to use it solely for HOWL business, within the access assigned to them. It is not offered for public registration, resale, or use by other businesses. HOWL may change or revoke access when business needs or personnel responsibilities change.

Accounts and confidential information

Use your own authorized account. Do not share sign-in credentials, bypass access controls, or disclose confidential company information to unauthorized people. Report suspected unauthorized access to the workspace owner promptly. Access to the Financials workspace and its QuickBooks connection is currently restricted to the owner.

QuickBooks connection

Only a person authorized to act for HOWL may connect its QuickBooks Online company. Campfire retrieves accounting reports for internal financial analysis, including revenue, costs, margins, break-even calculations, and target pacing. The implemented integration does not create or change QuickBooks transactions. Intuit's accounting authorization scope is broader than these implemented operations. Intuit's services remain subject to its own applicable terms.

The owner can disconnect the integration in Campfire and revoke its access in QuickBooks Connected Apps. Disconnecting removes Campfire's active connection credentials, saved report snapshot and cost classifications. Revenue targets and other manually entered financial plan settings remain.

Financial interpretation

Reports reflect the selected company, dates, accounting basis, and last successful sync. Targets and cost classifications are management inputs. Contribution margin, break-even, and pacing calculations depend on those inputs and their assumptions. Review them against the underlying books before making decisions. Campfire does not replace QuickBooks, accounting review, tax preparation, or professional advice.

Data and other company policies

Company records remain under HOWL's control. Users must follow applicable HOWL confidentiality, information-security, and records-handling requirements. The Campfire Financials Privacy Notice describes handling of QuickBooks data. These internal-use terms do not grant rights to use or disclose company records outside authorized HOWL business.

Support and changes

Contact the Campfire workspace owner for access, data-handling, or support requests. HOWL may update these terms and will make the current version available to authorized users. Any material expansion of QuickBooks data use or access must be reviewed before implementation.
